The repo already has precedents worth matching rather than re-deciding. card_part_sub renders “Front axle · ceramic” as “As depan · keramik”, and fit3d_bore_v establishes the comma decimal — ⌀ 54,1 mm, not 54.1. Whatever you write, those two conventions are already load-bearing.

Separately

The logo and the nav breakpoint are one decision, not two

This came out of the engineering review and it's the most useful thing my nav measurements produced — though not in the way I intended. My widths were taken on the proposed mark-plus-live-text lockup, not the baked image that's actually live, so the numbers didn't describe production and the recommendation was wrong. The founder ruled the other way and the bar fits 1040px today.

But the coupling is real: the six-link bar fits only because the baked lockup is narrow. Split the logo into mark + live wordmark — which is the only way to make the wordmark bigger, since the current asset has both baked into one 2891×1109 PNG — and the same six links run past 1040px. NAV_DESKTOP_QUERY has to move with it.

So don't rule on the logo as a brand question alone. Whatever lockup you pick, the bar gets re-measured against it before the constant changes. R16 Identity still holds the four directions if you want to revisit them.
