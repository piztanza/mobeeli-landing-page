BLOCKS CHANGE 8 Decision 1 — the nav has run out of room

Protection needs an anchor. There are already six links.

The bars below are built from the real parts — the actual mark at 42px, the wordmark at 32px Poppins 700, links at 14px/600, the same 26px and 22px gaps as the live nav — and the widths quoted are measured off these exact bars, not estimated. Check any of them in the console: $$('[data-bar]').map(e => e.dataset.bar + ' ' + e.offsetWidth).

Measuring changed the answer. The repo's breakpoint is 1040px, and once Protection is added no option clears it — not even dropping a link. So the bars are judged against 1109px, the raised breakpoint the R16 proposal already commits to. The real question is not whether to drop a link; it is how high the breakpoint has to go.
